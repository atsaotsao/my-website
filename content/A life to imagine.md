I imagine a life where I don't need to worry about doing the wrong thing or saying the wrong thing
Because there is no wrong thing 

I imagine a life where my flaws are like fireflies
They're not nuisance, or distractions, but floating lights that are as natural as the summer evening.

I imagine a life where my multitudes are all welcomed
Not just in my own body, but through every door in our home.

I imagine a life where I can break a bowl and we'll laugh
Because it reminds us just how precious our life and love is 
And because 歲歲平安 (suì suì píng ān)

I imagine a life where I can accidentally smudge the walls
And it's not a mistake, there's nothing to apologize for
Because walls are meant to be repainted.

I imagine a life where when I have days where I fall, fall hard on my knees
That I have a teammate patiently waiting at the next check point
Waiting to hug me.

I imagine a life where our kids don't need to worry about how loud they cry
Because we will not need to solve their pain, but will be there to hold them through it all.

I imagine this life for myself, because I never dared or gave myself the permission to imagine,
and because it meant asking the biggest and hardest question to the person I have loved the most in my lucky years on this earth:

Do you want this life as well?
