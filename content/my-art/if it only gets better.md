One of my practices with ink involves water and marbling patterns called *Suminagashi*. I cut up pieces of one particular print and paired them with gestural strokes of ink and red crayon, trying to hold the full texture of uncertainty, the not-knowing, the not-yet.

The gold vein running through it is the thing I keep coming back to. It feels like a thread you follow when you can't see where you're going. Surrender, for me, is choosing to trust that thread even when the you can't see what's on the other side. And to trust in the wisdom of the body, the land, the spirit, and the ancestors to get you to safe harbor.
