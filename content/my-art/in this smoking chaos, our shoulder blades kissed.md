I drew this piece right after the world started opening up after the pandemic in 2020-2021 to remind myself of the spaces of dancing and movement that I was craving. I was imagining bodies of energy and color, clustering and bouncing off of each other in a dark smoky room. Things may look hazy, foggy, or even at time disorienting, but it felt safe to weather it together. 

Looking at this painting again brings me back to the place where I took the simple joys of dancing my heart out with strangers for granted.  I hope this piece reminds you to move your body and dance, just as the beautiful person you are!
