One of the first pieces I ever drew and when in doubt, I always start with drawing with navy blue. I was thinking about clouds or glaciers. Whether it was fluffy or icy solid, both textures felt comforting for me. They both felt like they can carry a lot of fluids of sadness and that shape always made me feel more seen. I also wanted the red to represent the anger I move through, and the yellow optimism for a better life how it weaves through everything. They often coexist, meld together in a soup that I only just begun to make sense of earlier on in my journey. 

Hope it can be a compass, a mirror, or a reminder to embrace the shapes of where you are, regardless of how smooth or jagged they may feel.
