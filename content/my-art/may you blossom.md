When I found that I was going to become an uncle, I knew that I wanted to gift a piece of painting to my baby cousin. It was the best way I can think of to remind him of my love for him every day, even though I haven't met him yet and even though I may not be close by.

The monarch butterfly said hi to me one morning and I knew that I wanted to include him.

Just the idea of thinking him lying in his baby crib, looking up at this diptych and smiling; that just makes my heart full. I hope it brings protects him, guides him, and inspires him.
