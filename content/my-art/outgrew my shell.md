I don't think I ever considered myself a vision board kind of guy. Nor have I really done collaging ever.

But when I was decluttering for the new year, I found all these stacks of Wired and New Yorker magazines that I wanted to discard. But as I was literally carrying them out to the paper recycling, I could just hear them that they weren't ready to go yet. These magazines do play a pretty special role in my 20s since moving to New York City in 2013. I think I initially I got the monthly subscriptions because they made me feel like an "adult". I would always bring them to read on my daily almost hour long commute from Brooklyn all the way to Upper East Side.

In many ways, these magazines is the reason why I read, write, and draw. 
Maybe that's what the magazine were trying to tell me. They just wanted to be acknowledge, and even be included in my art process as well. 

This piece did open the doors for me in utilizing collaging combined with my other styles to create a layered depiction of my inner landscape. I hope it brings you a sense of expansion as it did for me when I created it.
