This wooden stick carries an inscription from Chapter 48 of the Tao Te Ching:

*為學日益，為道日損。損之又損，以至於無為。無為而無不為。取天下常以無事，及其有事，不足以取天下*

*In the pursuit of learning, every day something is gained. In the pursuit of the Tao, every day something is released. Released, and released again, until you arrive at non-doing. Through non-doing, nothing is left undone. Mastery of the world comes through non-interference. Those who strive and force their way are not fit to master the world.*

At the top sits a small sculpture made after being moved by Ruth Asawa's work, like a lantern at the tip of a staff. Something to light the way. Something to hold as you walk through.
