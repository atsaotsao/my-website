I spent a huge part of my life not noticing the sharp shapes and edges around me. 

Maybe because I grew up in it, it all just felt normal to me. Even when it pricked me, even when I bled, even when I got hurt (sometimes really hurt), I just accepted it and called it love. And I surrounded myself with people that made me feel that way. Because it was familiar with it. Maybe I was low-key even addicted to it. 

Part of the awakening journey for me is to see these sharp needles, and accept how all this have shaped me to who I am while still choosing to reimagine what life could be like without bleeding and bruising as much. Instead of blaming and pointing fingers, it’s about taking responsibility for all the things I can do, advocate for, change, and walk toward the love I know I deserve. 

This piece is as much about self love as it is about the challenges of being in relationships. 

There's a quote I found in a old journal entry that I have no idea where it came from: "There is no transformation unless it’s cellular and relational " This piece reminds me of the relational aspect of transformation. That all those sharp pointy edges that I've grazed against throughout my life is what led me to exactly where I am. 

And for that, I can grieve, rage, accept, and even be grateful of how it has shaped me. And hope it brings you some comfort in your journey as well.
