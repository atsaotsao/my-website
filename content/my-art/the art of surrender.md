_The Art of Surrender is an exhibition, a gathering, and a send-off._

_At the beginning of August 2026, I am leaving Brooklyn for Taiwan after thirteen years. I carry a Chinese name, grew up on Taiwanese soil, and yet spent most of my life looking toward the West. That journey gave me a language for personal freedom, but also revealed what is lost when we forget how to belong to something larger than ourselves._

_This show is what I am leaving behind. And what I am taking with me._

_The pieces move through grief, intergenerational trauma, and the ongoing work of reconnecting with land, spirit, and self. Surrender here is more than letting go. It is opening ourselves to receive what was always available: the gifts, the abundance, the love. And finally, choosing._
