A piece I drew for a friend who recently lost her dog.

I knew I wasn’t the kind of artist who could capture the dog’s realistic features, even with a good photo to reference, but I still wanted to make something that honored his spirit in an abstract way.

Just so happen, the mandarin translation name is 源氏. 

When I found out about that it immediately spoke to me. 
The character "源" means source, and I wanted to use that as the main focus.

I wanted to create a piece that I would want as a reminder when I'm grieving a loss.
To remind myself that my loved one has moved on to somewhere peaceful and beautiful

That they have returned back to the source of all things.
And that I can always find him/her there.

I hope this piece brings you the same comfort as well.comfort as well.
