My partner and I recently celebrated our seventh year of being together. She is one of the core reasons why this newsletter and these playlists exist in the first place. Not only is she my editor-in-chief who helps to look over every single issue to ensure they read well, but she is and always has been my biggest fan and supporter from the very beginning. Being in a long-term committed relationship has not always been easy, and we’ve definitely had our fair share of difficult days that forced us to examine the parts of ourselves that we don’t always like. However, being with her has not only made me more confident and empowered as an individual, but the love that we share has also helped me understand the more profound and noteworthy truths in life. Music is also way better when I’m enjoying it with her.

This month’s playlist is dedicated to her. It’s one of my signature-styled playlists, where I feature a diverse selection of musical styles and genres such as R&B, Indie Rock, Electronic, Soul, and Hip-hop. It highlights a variety of love songs that creatively express my deepest affection for and gratitude towards her. I would also like to remind and invite you to tell your loved one today, whether romantic or not, just how much they mean to you and how much you appreciate them.
