Being on the East Coast often means that I don’t have the luxury of easily attending music festivals like Coachella, but over the years I have used this time of the year to imagine what it would be like to host a festival of my own that spotlights the diverse range of Asian, Asian American, and Pacific Islander talent. If you haven’t already, you can check out [Part 1](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4a59uFErlAJfA4P4Z3w1Rc?si=f2c99dc220eb49e8) and [Part 2](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/3C8yRkK7su07Dy11xhO9AX?si=eefea5337df241d5), but if not, go ahead—put on your comfy music-listening pants and let’s go!

Right off the bat, I want to surprise you with an unexpected opener. Imagine how many more people would be willing to show up at the very beginning of the festival if this was the opener! Be prepared to be serenaded left and right by lush voices and groovy baselines. You’ve been warned. And before you plan to take a break, make sure you swing by the stage next door to catch an extraordinary display of rap abilities, especially the two artists that have traveled all the way from Korea and Taiwan. When the sun has set, and the lights are up, let’s go dance our asses off to the tune of B2B sets from some of the most badass Asian female DJs and producers in the business. The final slot of the night goes to Ryuichi Sakamoto and his ethereal and captivating music to end the day on a deeply moving note. I hope wherever you are, you can use this music to celebrate your day and week. Take care and until next time!
