Every year, to celebrate the end of AAPI month, I curate a playlist featuring some of my favorite Asian musicians creating the kind of magic that inspires me.

Over the past four years, I’ve chosen a less traditional professional path, recently even pivoting into [life coaching](https://andrew-tsao.com/?utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=i-had-a-dream-that-asian-musicians-took-over-coachella-part-4).

This journey often makes me reflect on those who dare to walk their path, believing it’s the right one for them.

I think about these musicians—though I don't know their personal stories, I can imagine the courage and optimism it took for them to keep creating and pushing forward.

When I listen to these tracks, even when they talk about heartbreak, I envision the perseverance and self-belief required to bring each song to life, from ideation to performance, recording, marketing, and finally, publishing on Spotify.

I channel that same energy and momentum to fuel my journey and reach my goals. I hope this playlist can offer you the same boost on your path.
