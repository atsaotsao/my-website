This month, you are all invited to my exclusive, extravagant music party playlist where I feature an assortment of music that will make you want to dance your socks off. First, I have a confession: I love the kind of electronic music that gets played in large music festivals, trendy concert venues, and even dark, gritty nightclubs, but I often hate being there. I live in New York City which means that at a lot of these events and venues, I have to deal with shirtless bros yelling every time the drop comes or being squished to the point where dancing isn’t even possible (looking at you @ [Terminal 5](https://www.instagram.com/terminal5nyc/?hl=en)). 

This is why it’s so deeply important to me that the set and setting of this particular night is perfectly curated. In this party that I am hosting, please imagine a multi-floored space, each floor the size of a tennis court, outfitted with its own state-of-the-art surround-sound speaker system. Every sustenance and substance need will be taken care of, free of charge. Everyone in attendance is someone you feel seen by, safe with, and comfortable to be around. It’s a Saturday night, and everyone is there for a shared intention: to dance to their heart's content. 

So please step on in, the party’s just started. For your convenience, you will find a personalized storage locker where we recommend leaving all of your belongings. We must also inform you that leaving your phone behind is a mandatory requirement; don’t worry, there’s wireless charging. When you enter, you will first be greeted with a few rounds of dreamy yet funky tunes to kick things off, quickly followed by poppy, melodic tunes to get you nice and warmed up. Follow me to the second floor and you will find a younger crowd already stationed there, banging their heads to soundtracks of angst with a thrashy, glitchy quality to it at a volume that feels like it will break the speakers at any moment now. *Hyperpop* is what the kids are calling it these days. Moving on to the next room—this is where things start to take off. You will begin to hear how different artists express an electronic sound in a variety of creative ways. Some pair beats with soothing arcs of melodies, some unconventional glitches with unique synths, while others use tempo changes to fill the room and your ears with as much beauty, freedom, and chaos as you can handle. As you keep climbing, the beat will get louder, faster, and stronger. If you can make it to the end of the night and to the top floor, join me in watching the sunrise together, savoring the last few drops of a fun night where we got to fully just be.
