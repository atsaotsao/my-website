A little over a week ago, my fiancée and I went to the [Fred again..](https://open.spotify.com/artist/4oLeXFyACqeem2VImYeBFe?si=PbR8hpZ6Q9Of2-dPTuey8g) concert at Forest Hills Stadium in Queens, NY. Ever since I started my entrepreneurship journey, I’ve had less disposable income than when I had a full-time job which means that I no longer spend as much money on concert tickets as before; but when I do, it’s usually for something very important and meaningful for both of us. 

The weather was pretty dreadful on the day of the concert. It was cold and raining all day, and on top of that, we had an hour-and-a-half commute to a venue that was going to be outdoors. Despite all that, it turned out to be one of the most important concert experiences I’ve ever had. Ever since I moved to New York in July 2013, I’ve been to a total of 113 concerts, but this concert has been and will forever be one of the most memorable ones. I still remember the moment that did it for me. As Fred played [this track](https://open.spotify.com/track/7c584s9RZQzkJDoC08VDJB?si=5f86d3d62427493b) and the words “I just know that it gets better with time” rang across the stadium with over 10,000 people dancing ecstatically, I was instantly transported back in time to my younger self in college and remembered just how lonely that boy felt. I realized at that moment that one of the reasons that I was always listening to so much music and going to so many concerts was that I was often searching for something— something that would make me feel so alive that even a depressed kid like me would feel like life had meaning and was worth living. Standing there with the ocean of emotions washing over me, I felt so much joy and pride— for the moment and for the person I’ve become since.

I want to bring you along to the concert and the feeling that was in the air that night with this month’s playlist. I’ve included a compilation of some of my favorite dance tracks that somehow make me want to both dance and cry at the same time. I’ve learned over these couple of years that sometimes shaking your body and moving it with complete freedom (dancing it out) can not only make you feel happier and energized, but it can also help release a lot of the emotions that are held and trapped in your body. So with this playlist, I invite you to join along with me and release anything that you’re holding onto. Whatever you’re feeling, always remember that you are not alone, and know that it really does get better with time.
