For the past months, I’ve been encouraging myself to break the rules.

To examine the rules I have in my head about identity and creativity, and find ways to courageously fiddle with it, warp it, push and pull on it, and even completely shatter it.

There’s a Mandarin character I think about a lot which is the word “乖”, or “obedient.”

Growing up in Taiwan, this word is often thrown around by my parents and grandparents.

Based on your behaviors, grades, and overall politeness, you will often be put under the bucket of 乖 or 不乖 (not obedient).

But I realized that to understand myself better, I needed to start fully embracing and loving parts of my selves that were given that label at an early age of 不乖.

This month’s curation is a reflection of the examination of what it means to unlearn the rules imposed on us to learn more about who we truly are.

I was deeply inspired by Jacob Collier’s recent album [Djesse Vol.4](https://open.spotify.com/album/13r6eqjYlKELFQlNvVCBz1?si=dq5WNq4VS2GZiNEsTX87kQ&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=be-who-you-are) and Porter Robinson’s newest single [Cheerleader](https://open.spotify.com/track/779mhz1mAry946r91qzR1r?si=28d268980c534076&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=be-who-you-are).

Listening to these artists continue to redefine their sounds while staying true to how they express their worlds made me want to do the same.

So here’s a playlist that is unlike any you will find via the Spotify curation or algorithmic recommendation, where I jam-packed as many of my favorite songs about the exploration of the self across multiple genres.

From Rock to Rap, Indie Soul to Indietronica, you may find yourself weaving through uncharted waters

But if you are willing to trust me, I will always bring you back to dry, safe, and warm grounds. Just enjoy the ride.
