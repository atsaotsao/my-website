I have some close friends who have recently gone through a breakup. Hearing them open up about their emotions and struggles really brought me back to the headspace of my younger self. It took me a long time before I could admit to myself that I was a really sensitive kid growing up, especially compared to other guys when it came to love and relationships. I still remember how I would spend hours on end listening to music on my portable CD player (I used to rock this [bad boy](https://www.amazon.com/Philips-AX5210-Compact-Portable-Player/dp/B00009P3MF) wherever I go) and contemplate love—love gained, love lost, and everything in between. I learned early on that the most powerful thing about love songs is that in those vulnerable moments of my life, they felt like the only things that could capture the full scope of my emotions. In many ways, they made me feel seen and comforted when in the depths of a massive teenage heartbreak over life or love.

This month’s curation is dedicated to anyone who has recently experienced a breakup or heartbreak. Drawing on an array of genres including indie rock, R&B, and dream pop, I wanted to host a comfortable space where you feel like you can kick back on a weekday night with a glass of wine or tea and dip your toes into the waters of any difficult emotions you may have been avoiding lately; trust that the music will carry you and you’ll be able to swim safely in any emotion that arises. If you are like me and have a partner that you love, this space could also help facilitate a time to be with feelings of deep love and gratitude. At the end of the day, it’s always important to remind ourselves that _we may be lonely, but we're never that alone._ And if you ever need someone to talk to, just know that I will always be an email away :)
