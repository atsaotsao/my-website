Before we proceed with a new year’s intention, allow me to invite you to take a moment to relax, immerse yourself in the soothing beats of my playlist, and begin the year at a leisurely pace. I myself have just returned from visiting my family in Taiwan, and then a wedding in Dallas, and am now excited for the opportunity to get settled back into my routine and prepare myself to take on the year ahead. This month’s ~flavor~ is predominantly lo-fi. If you’re someone who’s ever looked up a playlist to work or study to either on Spotify or Youtube (all hail [lo-fi girl](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfKfPfyJRdk&ab_channel=LofiGirl)!) or have frequented vlogs, then this month’s sound should feel very familiar. Something about the combination of the 80 to 110 beats per minute tempo, jazzy elements, and even the occasional inclusion of iykyk audio samples always conjures up a feeling of nostalgia, intimacy, and rawness for me that gets me right in the zone.

This month’s curation is all about helping you ease into whatever you’ve got going on. Don’t be afraid to lean on the lush sounds of the saxophone to get you through a particularly stressful afternoon, or allow the low, hazy hum of beats to move you into a flow state while tackling projects and tasks you’ve set your intentions on. We have a new year ahead of us but don’t forget to warm up, stretch, and invite some softness in before you start sprinting toward your goals. Sometimes all we need is a little permission to ease into things along with the courage to get started, so let’s do this, together!
