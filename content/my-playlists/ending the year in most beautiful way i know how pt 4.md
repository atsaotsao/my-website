Ever since I started making these playlists in 2017, my goal has always been to amplify the beauty of the music that I have encountered and experienced. It’s an extension of the gratitude that I have for the musical education I received growing up, the concerts I’ve attended, the albums I’ve spent countless hours listening to, the friends I’ve enjoyed them with, and the artists who pour their hearts and souls into this work. It’s through these tracks that I am able to survive, be, and thrive. 

This month’s curation is all about diving into and swimming in the tranquil sounds of piano and classical music. This is the fourth year in a row now that I’m closing out the year this way (check out: [Part 1](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4VcLYn7O0CxMG5cAxuoMe0?si=8906663483c84cea), [Part 2](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/79TFc0aobJuj2yhvHljBzv?si=4146a0a3388248ec), or [Part 3](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/6pm2bEGu4RS38gwzPspu9w?si=9f56841d78cb4036) if you’ve missed them) because I have always felt like it’s the best time in the year to slow down a little and reflect on our growth, pay attention to our emotions, and celebrate the wins, however big or small they may be. The music is meant to be paired with any quiet time of introspection you could find for yourself between the holiday celebrations. Even if you are not planning to do an annual review or make a list of new year's resolutions or goals, I want to invite you to use at least the length of any song on this playlist to close your eyes, breathe, and simply focus on the feeling of gratitude for the unbelievable fact that we are still here—alive, well, and hopefully smiling. Whether this year has been easy or not, I hope you find the joy, love, and courage to embrace the ups & downs and keep going. New year, same amazing you ✨ You got this! 💪
