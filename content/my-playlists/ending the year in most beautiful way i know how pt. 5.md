This is the 5th year in the row I ended my year with a playlist like this, where I pick 25 of some of the most beautiful instrumental and classical tracks that have really touched me. As a kid who grew up listening to my mom teaching others how to play piano in the next room, the sound of the piano always reminds me of childhood and home. Over the years, I realized that it's the sound of my heart and it has always been my go-to frequencies when I feel lost, unseen, and unsafe in the world. And now, listening to them always reminds me of how far I've come and how far I want to go.

This month's curation is more than just about reflection, it's also about acceptance and expansion. I organized the songs in a way where you can hear a range of different styles of piano playing. Through the jazzy sounds of [FKJ](https://open.spotify.com/artist/2FwDTncULUnmANIh7qKa5z?si=ZAaL3IJfTHSK-p-zKE-rgA&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=ending-the-year-in-the-most-beautiful-way-i-know-how) & [Elijah Fox](https://open.spotify.com/artist/4Rus30xX4FOv2cyeFI79Qh?si=RawqY-uOT069qd2bUkQHuQ&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=ending-the-year-in-the-most-beautiful-way-i-know-how), to the hauntingly beautiful chords of [Hania Rani](https://open.spotify.com/artist/14YzutUdMwS9yTnI0IFBaD?si=rbPdIneyQ_6egIeYnr4wxA&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=ending-the-year-in-the-most-beautiful-way-i-know-how) & [Olafur Arnalds](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7E3BRXV9ZbCt5lQTCXMTia?si=9tqpLql3ROmlTT2bAEiCoQ&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=ending-the-year-in-the-most-beautiful-way-i-know-how), and finally to the transcendent ambience of [Daigo Hanada](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7kxdoQTibsQW5pOim1p2i3?si=zQm9CrbrRQKRIDnOv1zoTA&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=ending-the-year-in-the-most-beautiful-way-i-know-how) & [Alaskan Tapes](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5GHBk4xcO1UqlbyrUXv5dq?si=qqfQAQs7SH6cM-Hzc7HWkg&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=ending-the-year-in-the-most-beautiful-way-i-know-how), I hope to showcase a range so you can appreciate the playfulness, melancholy, brightness, and serenity of the melodies. Also, sandwiched in the middle are also some of my favorite tracks from soundtracks I adore.

If this kind of music, especially ones where it's predominantly just a piano playing, sounds like just “sad” music to you, then I invite you to use this opportunity to listen to it in a new way. I encourage you to find a pocket of alone and quiet time during these last few days of the year where you can close your eyes and allow yourself to swim in the sounds. Instead of just labeling the sorrow, try broadening the musical lens and hear both the happy and the sad, the good and the bad, the shadow and the light. And through embracing the duality and tension in these notes and the emotions they may bring up, I hope you can take away something to help you accomplish all that you believe you can in the upcoming year. I’m rooting for you! 💪
