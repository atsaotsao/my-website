This is the third year in a row I am ending the year with an entire playlist dedicated to the delicate beauty of piano, because in many ways, it’s an ode to my mom. I owe a lot of my appreciation of music to her and how she’s shown me this world at an early age. In [Part 1]([https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4VcLYn7O0CxMG5cAxuoMe0?si=0aee7ef1cc4b4e0e](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4VcLYn7O0CxMG5cAxuoMe0?si=0aee7ef1cc4b4e0e)), I mostly wanted to feature a selection of all my favorite pianists in one place. The next year, in [Part 2]([https://25on25.webflow.io/](https://25on25.webflow.io/)), I created a web experience where I featured two amazing writers that I know and paired them with tracks I’d selected.

This year for Part 3, I hope to convey something that transcends the summation of 25 classical tracks. If you listen closely and patiently all the way until the end, while keeping your ears and heart open, you will hear some important lessons I’ve learned this year; lessons that have made me a better and happier person, and I am hoping to pass on those learnings in the best way I can.

The new year always breeds new opportunities. You just have to listen closely.

I’ll see you next year!
