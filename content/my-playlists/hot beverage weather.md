In my household growing up, I’ve always been advised to drink hot beverages over cold ones, but never really understood why. Keeping in mind that I grew up in Taiwan, which is geographically just a little north of the equator, what this meant was that we lived in an extremely humid climate. Moisture hanging heavy in the air and clinging to my skin is something I am deeply familiar with. As a kid who was always moving and chasing after a ball, I was often drenched in sweat and found myself craving an ice cold beverage in my hand. It wasn’t until recently that I understood the meaning of the old adages I heard all throughout my childhood. When thinking about what you want vs. what you need, a warm beverage actually works more harmoniously with your body, even when you feel like you are burning up, simmering in your own sweat and would rather jump into an imaginary pool of iced tea.

As we’re entering the winter season, it’s much easier for us to be attuned to our bodies’ need for heat. In lower temperatures, as much as we delight in the change of seasons, our bodies ultimately crave heat in any form. A hot beverage is a reminder to listen to our needs and to find a way to give ourselves more of what we not only want, but need. It’s a way for us to restore balance within us and with it, our health.

This month’s playlist is all about helping you to restore some balance to your life. The upcoming holidays means that you may finally have the luxury of taking a few days off from work and your usual routined life, and I want to help fill it with just a little dose of warmth and feel-goodness. Through a selection of jazz-infused tracks along with R&B ballads and velvety voices, I am hoping to wrap you in a blanket of soothing sonic satisfaction that makes you feel safe and loved. Whether you are spending the holidays at home or away, alone or with others, I hope that the music brings some joy to your world.
