I started this year with a deep session of introspection.

Even though I’ve found some success in my coaching business in the past two years, there’s a voice in me that is nudging me to evolve.

I once heard Oprah say in an interview that: _"Your life speaks to you in whispers."_

The more I dive into my consciousness, through practices of mindfulness and meditation, the more I notice myself starting to hear these whispers.

This month, I decided to choose the type of music that can pair with that intention of diving deeper within myself, learning to be selfish to protect my peace, and hoping to find all the right answers and wisdom within me, to push me in the direction I need to go.

I hope that it can also be a helpful soundtrack to wherever you are on your journey right now.
