One of my favorite aspects of listening to music is that it's the closest thing we get to time traveling. I still remember vividly when I first saw the movie *Drive* in 2011 at a theater in LA and was completely blown away by the [soundtrack and music supervision]([https://open.spotify.com/album/3fKas0iq7DWAylIQxGEIQW?si=WflqV-xEQ4uAgSI9i4RvHA&dl_branch=1](https://open.spotify.com/album/3fKas0iq7DWAylIQxGEIQW?si=WflqV-xEQ4uAgSI9i4RvHA&dl_branch=1)). The retro and synth-driven melody arcs reminiscent of the 80s were a sound I was unfamiliar with (I was born in the early 90s) and was instantaneously hooked by. When *Stranger Things* came out in 2016, the [title sequence]([https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-RcPZdihrp4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-RcPZdihrp4)) was so memorable that the sounds of "synthwave" and "retrowave" have since been part of my steady diet of music.

This month's curation is a tribute to my love for these sounds. Listening to this music conjures images of retro-postcards: carefree summers spent driving along the coastline. If you listen closely, you can also hear the 80s' love affair with science and technology, embodied in classic sci-fi movies with images of neon lights, video games, flying supercars, and futuristic landscapes. You don't have to be driving or even commuting home to let this playlist take you on a ride, as far away as you'd like to go.
