I’ve been giving a lot of space to myself in the past weeks to allow and feel the rising emotions in my body. In the past, I would often try to find a lot of ways to distract myself and bypass it, or channel it into areas where I can do something that feels more “productive.” But my body has a lot he wants to say, and I still learning to just listen and let it be.

This month’s curation is a little different from what I usually would do as it’s heavily inspired by the music I listened to 10 years ago (from [this playlist](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/48VEm87WaT5nAxmTGy7GzJ?si=8870bf0ee3a4425c)). Even though there is a lot of sadness, I felt a lot of comfort and safety swimming in a lot of these songs while reading the lyrics, so I hope it can bring you some warmth as well. See you next month!
