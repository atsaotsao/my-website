This month’s curation started with me trying to find songs to keep myself awake and I’m happy to share that it definitely worked. One minute I could barely keep my eyes open and the next I was bopping around in my bedroom, unpacking and successfully battling against my yawns. As time passed, the deeper I yearned for the sounds to be, and the more I was able to take in the fact that it’s been almost two years since I’ve been back in Taiwan. It feels so damn good to be home again. Wherever you are, I hope you feel at home too!
