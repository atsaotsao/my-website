One of the most powerful aspects of music is how it allows us to have this superpower of manipulating time. A song can transport us back in time, like how listening to [this song](https://open.spotify.com/track/0F02KChKwbcQ3tk4q1YxLH?si=cda172c5c6344864) immediately brings me back to when I was in middle school trying to do my math homework instead of just obsessing over my crush. It can even pause or slow down time. 

This past summer, I’ve been busy building and running my coaching business, while learning to export more and create more content. Reflecting back on these past few months, I realize that I didn’t really get a lot of opportunities to do something else I really enjoy, which is to be out dancing. So this playlist is my own attempt to keep the summer going for just a little bit longer so I can dance my heart out before it gets cooler again.

This month’s curation is all about keeping that summer spirit alive, even if it’s just for the duration of a playlist in our homes and living rooms. I’m starting things off nice and easy with the likes of artists like Panthurr and ConsciousThoughts to get ourselves warmed up. Use this moment to get yourself nice and loose, because the funk is coming! The tracks are gonna get groovier by the second, and the beat will keep going as we transition from disco and nu-disco to more modern electronic tunes. Finally, I’m closing the evening with a handful of worldly dance songs that will transport you to a space where you feel like you are dancing with people from all over the world, reminding us that regardless of where we are, we are all in it together.
