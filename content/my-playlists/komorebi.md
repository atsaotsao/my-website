Ever since the school year started, my girlfriend, who is a teacher at a charter school here in Brooklyn, and I have been waking up at 6 am every day (some days even earlier.) It's this time of day when the sun is usually barely peeking through the clouds and a ray of sunshine hits straight across my living room, as if gently guiding me to my first cup of morning coffee. In the haziness and grogginess of dawn, I also find myself craving the kind of sound that simultaneously exudes the warmth of my duvet and the exciting anticipation of crispy hash browns for breakfast.

This month's curation is inspired by that craving and also the Japanese word, komorebi. Komorebi is one of those words that can't be translated into English, but it refers to the unique spectacle one enjoys when sunlight is filtered by the web of tree branches and leaves. What it creates isn't a lesser version of the sunlight itself, but something more complex, layered and magical. It illuminates the world in a way that makes everything seem simply more... tolerable. This feeling of hopeful tranquility is conveyed through the majority of tracks in this playlist through the underlying emotional arc of soothing piano sounds paired with the variations of beats. It is my wish that those beats can help set the rhythm for you as you begin your day, while reminding you that each day is filled to the brim with potential.
