For a long time, I realized that I was spending the bulk of my life waiting for permission: to learn, to realize my potential, to be joyful, and to express myself fully.  I can’t tell you the exact moment when it clicked, but I truly started giving myself the permission to be, to play, and to show up as my full self. I started sharing my music curations in 2017 and then writing about them in 2020. I started drawing and playing the piano. I started a business. I started to understand stillness through my meditation practice. And these past weeks, I have also started giving myself permission to understand my body, the weight that I carry, and the stiffness and pain in my upper back through movement and dance. 

This month’s curation is another personal one, as it’s one that contains a lot of the feelings and love I want to share with myself and with anyone who is reading & listening. It’s one that is brimming with songs that make me want to cry, laugh, and dance all at the same time. They all feel like fuel to my soul and they remind me to come back to my Self. I hope it can do the same for you as well.
