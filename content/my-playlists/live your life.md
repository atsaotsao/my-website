I don’t take warm days for granted anymore.

When it comes springtime, sometimes in the morning, when I see a ray of sunshine landing in my living room, I will drag my meditation mat directly under it to sit while facing the sun.

As I bask in the warm glow of the sun, I feel so safe and loved.

I sometimes like to imagine it feeling like the universe putting a gentle hand on my back, or her wrapping her hands around me, and letting me know that she’s always there for me.

And it’s okay for me to go forth and live the life I’ve always wanted.

This month’s curation, with the help of the jazzy and cozy tunes, I’m encouraging y’all to step out of your homes and go take yourself on a long walk or on a solo date that you so deserve.

And live the life you’ve always wanted. I’ll see you next month.
