If you have been following my playlists for a long time, then you may have started to pick up on the fact that there are a few select artists that fall under my list of favorites. Whenever the opportunity arises, I always find a way to sneak one of their songs into my playlist in the hopes that more people will know about these artists and discover something in their music that speaks to them. I’m honest enough to admit that I secretly want to convert all of my subscribers into fanboys and fangirls of my favorite artists. One of these artists that I deeply admire is Porter Robinson. He's one of those artists that I’ve been following since the beginning of his career. I still remember when he released his first album, Worlds, in 2014 when I was in my second year of living in New York and still struggling to find my footing in the chaos that is this city. I still insisted on gathering my friends to have an album listening party. Fast forward to two days ago, when Porter finally released his sophomore album, and as a treat to his fans, he even hosted a free virtual music festival where everyone is gathered in a gorgeous VR world to watch him & his musician friends perform the entire day. After already spending hours listening to the new album on repeat when it dropped on Friday, and then dancing dorkily in front of TV throughout the virtual festival on Saturday afternoon, I knew that my playlist would have to be heavily inspired by the album and the experience. 

For this month’s playlist, I selected songs from a variety of artists that performed in the virtual festival, while making sure to maximize the range of electronic music styles featured. You’ll have an opportunity to hear how different electronic musicians find their own creative ways to produce layered soundscapes that may not sound like the typical EDM-driven tracks you hear on the radio. I’ve also curated the songs to really build as they go, so if you’ve never listened to a track that is over 124 beats per minute, strap in, because you’re in for a ride. Enjoy!
