I started listening to electronic music when I first attended university in the early 2010s.

The genre’s popularity was largely influenced by the American music industry and music press, and specifically their efforts to rebrand American rave cultures.

Back then, whenever I went to a rave, I always just thought that it was a giant party.

And I kind of missed the point.

Looking back now, I finally realized that behind the loud music, the visuals, the shuffling, the candies, and the outfits were something much deeper and grander.

Rave has always been a kind of collective healing.

Through the synchronized beats and dancing, I realized that it allowed me to participate in a communal and ceremonial form of celebration of all that is beautiful in life; of love and of unity of the human spirit.

These raves helped me heal from my depression, even in ways I weren’t even conscious of and I will always be grateful for them.

This month’s curation, and [part 1](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/26BSkSgpBn6XRjpicccyzX?si=f1aa8bd2df79472a&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=optimistically-electronic), are love letters to the kinds of electronic music that remind me of the optimism I have for my life and this world.

As you journey further into the playlist, you will not only enjoy a variety of styles and increasing tempos but also a whole range of expressions of beauty.

Don’t be surprised if you feel and feels come up while listening through.

I hope these songs can move you, both physically and emotionally, as they have for me!
