Welcome to the 54th issue of my monthly music newsletter

I’m a day late I know, but you know what I’m INCREDIBLY proud of? This is the only the second time ever in the span of 7 years that I didn’t send this newsletter out on the 25th.

So, I have some news for y’all…

After much reflection, I’ve decided that the next few months will be the final installments of my monthly playlist.

As someone who’s embracing my multi-passionate creative path, it was really important to take a hard look at where I spend my time.

Right this moment, I am currently building a [coaching practice](https://andrew-tsao.com/?utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=recapping-7-years&_bhlid=1cb2ed31f33881c86885985f14bf2a202349acd2), creating [abstract art](https://www.instagram.com/daily.abstraction/?utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=recapping-7-years&_bhlid=b093b1cefde34023f27a560c649c217926ecd1c8), producing [short-form content](https://www.instagram.com/atsaotsao/?utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=recapping-7-years&_bhlid=cb910f552f96fae28c1eadc031d2b665cd81c9f5) every day, while also running a [podcast show](https://joyofbuilding.transistor.fm/?utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=recapping-7-years&_bhlid=37ab4dc12825f8aa4cfcb663d9e90878b40ee862).

And the honest truth is that I don’t consume as much music and in the same way as I have before.

I no longer spend hours of my time scouring playlists and music blogs for the newest and freshest tracks, but instead would often resort to carving out intentional time when I can to be fully present and listen to an album from beginning to end.

And ever since I’ve started sharing on stories on Instagram this year, the ability to include music or share music directly from Spotify into IG has been just a game changer in me being able to express to others how I hear music and how it relates to how I feel or interact with this world.

The music I listen to and share, have and will always be a deep part of my being, authenticity, expression, love, and light.

That’s why this monthly space has been so special to me.  
Without this playground, without these words, without this format, without you reading or listening to my curation, I would not have been able to get to where I am today.

I still have two more issues to express my gratitude so I’ll just stop here for now.

This month I included some of the favorite tracks from my monthly curations for the past 7 years. Enjoy!

With gratitude,  
Andrew
