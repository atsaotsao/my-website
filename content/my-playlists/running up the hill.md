I recently finished binging the latest season of the Netflix show _Stranger Things_ and have not been able to stop thinking about it ever since. One of the reasons I have so much love for this show is because of the significant influence it has had on my music taste. I still remember back in 2016 when the first season debuted, the moment the [title sequence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-RcPZdihrp4&ab_channel=StrangerThings) started playing, I immediately fell in love with the style and sound. That moment, paired with just fantastic music supervision for the entirety of the show, sent me on an excavation mission to find and understand this kind of music. You see, as a kid who grew up with classical instruments and mando-pop in Taiwan in the early 90s, the 80s synth-heavy music felt completely alien but intriguing to me. The most memorable, early exposures I had to these sounds were from watching the movies _Blade Runner_ and _Drive_, but it was this show that ignited a deep curiosity in me that not only sent me on a long journey of discovering amazing genres and subgenres like synthwave, vaporwave, and synth-pop, but also influenced my early intentions of thoughtful curation and sharing of music. 

This month’s playlist is all about sharing that love with all of you. It’s not just a love letter to the show, but to the synths that have helped shaped my life. (Here are some more past “love letters” to the sound: [_Eleven_](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/2RjAV07scaoHkNjp6RwF6w?si=668fb30ca959473a)_,_ [_Synth U Been Gone_](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/2FSyEXVcAUBm6jBexWyoPZ?si=e589d6dd8d664bf0)_,_ [_In the Moog for Love_](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/7jpjnQtgQ1t862AS8vHZjI?si=7f564f2a1e7949af)_,_ [_Trapped in a Video Game_](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/29nHmcfrHo3OO4f0MR6DQE?si=d30abc1f78b944ab)_,_ [_Into The Setting Sun_](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/2HTFUtElblW2upCdGymta1?si=2b08805f0ba2454c)). For this month in particular, in addition to picking songs that are direct nods to _Stranger Things_, I also picked an assortment of styles to showcase how much the sound that sent me on rabbit chase over the years, can be found in so many types of music, even in mainstream pop today. If this isn’t your cup of tea, as your fellow internet music friend, I implore you to be open and listen to the playlist from beginning to end and try to discover the warmth and beauty behind the computer-generated notes, because I promise you that it will only get better in time. In the famous words of Eleven from the show, “Friends don’t lie.”
