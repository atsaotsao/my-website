The name of this month’s theme is named after one of my favorite musician’s [newest album](https://open.spotify.com/album/3RyNOK2vXaGdkRWoh8QmjN?si=AlbjsfqERkq9AuRuVuQvCw) which came out yesterday! I still haven’t listened to it as I plan to have a mini-listening party this weekend. Out of curiosity, when was the last time you hosted or went to an album-listening party? 🙂

When I think about songs to come home to, I think about things that make me feel safe, warm, and all fuzzy inside—songs that fill my cup at the end of a long day and allow me to have the courage to take on the rest of the day, week, and month. There are a lot of songs that convey that feeling for me. Recently when I was at the beach with my partner, there was a couple that was playing music from their speaker, and the vibe instantly enveloped me in this feeling and inspired a lot of my song selection on the playlist this month. 

I realize that home is a feeling. Even though I’m 7,822.27 miles away from where I grew up and where my parents are, I’ve learned to create a home here and wherever I go. I hope this playlist can make you feel like that as well.
