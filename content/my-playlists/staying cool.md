In a recent conversation with a friend, I’m reminded of what it’s like to be single in New York during the summer. I’ve been in a committed relationship for more than 6 years now and I didn’t even realize how much I’d forgotten about the emotions and actions that come along with the search for love and companionship, especially during a time of the year and in a place like this where you may be constantly forced to deal with the paradox of choice: the abundance of options and choices require all the more effort to make decisions, something that can ultimately leave you feeling more likely than not unsatisfied with your choice.

This month’s playlist is dedicated to all of you single folks out there. I handpicked a selection of some of my recent favorite indie rock, bedroom pop, and R&B songs for you to play on a hot day. Allow yourself to let go of any FOMO and take a full day where you give yourself permission to stay at home with the AC on and blast some music while enjoying your own company. Whether you are recently single, dating around, still figuring things out, or somewhere in between, I hope you use this time to enjoy the simple pleasures this season can offer.
