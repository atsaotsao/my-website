One of my favorite ways to listen to music is to picture myself in space, on a vessel flying around in a world so vast and beautiful. This month I've decided to bring you along for the ride.

Sitting in my spacecraft, wearing my favorite summer outfit and observing the world beneath, I often feel like I'm all alone against the large backdrop of uncertainty. I am just a dot on this large tapestry painted with predetermined brush strokes; that's simultaneously so distressing and comforting to submit to. There's so much beauty in the infinitesimally small things, both by itself and in its ability to be grouped into larger and more vibrant colors. Just like the notes that exist between the repetitiveness of the thumps and beats on this playlist, if you listen closely, each individual note may not be that interesting, but with its complementing melodies, it creates something so mesmerizing that it puts you in a hypnotic trance.

This month, the song choices will lean heavily on the electronic genres of music and I've curated two parts to this journey. In the first part, we will take a full tour circling around the earth where the sounds are brighter and more lyrical. In the second part, we will dive into the depths of space where the tracks get more dissonant and layered with deeper sounds. For anyone not ready for the second part of the journey, please feel free to hop off at Track #10, at Corners Of the Earth.

When you're ready, put on your space suits, and let's go! And don't worry, we'll be back in time for an ice-cold margarita at my rooftop party.
