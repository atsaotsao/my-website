For as long as I can remember, I’ve been obsessed with morning routines. This is especially funny to those who know me well, because throughout my life I’ve consistently struggled with not being able to wake up in the morning. I think everyone likes to claim that they are “not a morning person,” but I would challenge anyone to owning a more impressive track record than I do of not waking up in the morning and experiencing the resulting consequences of it. Just ask my friend Daniel. One time he had to wait an extra hour and twenty minutes just for our bowling man-date. Or ask my professor of Communications, who politely emailed me “Where were you?” after I failed to show up to my Midterm exam.

Over the years, I’ve chased after this elusive definition of what mornings mean to me, through iterations of personal experiments: tapping into different ways of motivating myself, trying out different reward systems, and obsessing over habit-building. Truth be told, it took this year of sitting in the park reading, meditating, and then saying to myself, “Boy, I wish I could do this and feel like this every day” to realize the kind of mornings I want to have in perpetuity, and why they matter. I realize that to be the best versions of ourselves, our mornings are the single most important thing that help us to get there.

For this month’s playlist, I handpicked a selection of jams to help you start your mornings right; it’s got just the right amount of groove, funk, and jazz to get you feeling loosened up, comfortable, and all-around lively. Here’s me wishing your days, weeks, and months ahead look even brighter from here.
