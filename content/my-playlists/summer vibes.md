If you're ever wondering what New York is like on a breezy summer night, I advise you to visit Washington Square Park after dinner, find a cozy park bench and just sit and watch people interact with each other. The energy is absolutely electric, and I created this playlist as an attempt to capture that scenery. I combined the electronic goodness of Anoraak, Jai Wolf, and Moon Boots, with the funkiness of FKJ and SG Lewis. Sandwiched between genres is "All About Reggie" a phenomenal jazz song taken from the Netflix show "Dear White People." Also, I feel it would be a crime to not include Arcade Fire's new song "Everything Now." Finally, the playlist ends with my favorite song from Lorde's new album.

Enjoy!
