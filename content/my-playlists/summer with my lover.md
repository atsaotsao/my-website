I realize that music is one of my love languages.

Ever since I started dating Laureen more than eight years ago, I’ve always shared music with her and taken her to live concerts.

It’s because, before I learned to find the language to communicate my inner world and the ocean of emotions I’m feeling, music was one of the ways I could share that world with her.

To let her know how much her love, her being, and her presence every day have moved and changed me.

And even after eight years, even though I tell her “I love you” every single day, sometimes nothing conveys the extent of my love for her better than the songs in this month’s playlist.

I also love that she often saves my playlists to listen to on her walks or in the shower.

This newsletter would not have existed without you. So thank you, Laureen, for everything.
