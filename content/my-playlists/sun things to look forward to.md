It's a weird time to be listening to music right now with all that's going on around us. In fact, all the data shows that the amount of music streaming activity has been declining since the pandemic started, but I'm still here with a list of hand-selected songs to remind you of the important things: to stay optimistic about what's ahead of us and hold on dear to all the warm and beautiful things we still look forward to.

Since this is the first time I'm sharing my playlist via a newsletter, I want to use the opportunity re-introduce what it's all about and give you a taste of the music that gets featured. This playlist wouldn't just be like any other playlists you follow; it will always contain 25 songs on the 25th of every month and each of them are curated to be listened in order and would always revolve around a central theme: a mood, a pun, a imagery, or even a story. You will always expect to hear more than one genre of music. Finally, I will always end with a classical song that I love because, you know, to stay classy. 

This month is a smorgasbord of music genres that I like the most and often feature the most: from indie pop to vapor soul,electronic bangers to modern rock tracks, and from synthy chillwave vibes to minimal neoclassical soundscapes, the playlist is here to get you out of your funk, out of your seat, and to your happy place.

Enjoy!
