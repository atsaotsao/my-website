Welcome to the 55th & final issue of my monthly music newsletter and my 90th monthly custom-curated playlist.

I started my [very first](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/6Qrto1qxGjdOiqLrZhrYfD?si=ffb1f36839f348ec&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=56eafbedbef4b7b2f5a58686f4bb0942a5bc170f) playlist curation in April 2017 and I don’t think I ever imagined myself doing it for 7 more years.

This journey has been everything—just everything to me.

It taught me a lot about what it took to be consistent

It gave me a structure and incentive to listen to, explore, and interact with music in a way my inner child always craved.

It showed me what it could feel like to do something that my soul wanted, even if it didn’t make financial or rational sense to most people.

It gifted me the indescribable joy and peace of having those efforts of love not just received but mirrored back to me.

As I’m writing this on Christmas eve, I’m feeling extremely touched and grateful.

I remember how proud I felt when a friend came to me in real life and told me, “I listen to your playlist every month.”

I remember how good it felt when my mom shared with me, “I don’t think I understood electronic music but through this most recent curation, I think I get it more now.”

I remember receiving an email from a subscriber I’ve never met sharing how much a a particular curation meant to her.

As I look back on these months, and the specific themes I had like 🌴 [Some Mermaid Forest](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/6gvuqarODYyqVJ8IFtWrYw?si=009e0cee50274e0c&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=c7642fbf619cc8848e61d2ad4edf843562ac1446), [☀️](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/0L8dak6CDIECehyShf2Vhb?si=ea2801aeecab4d6a&nd=1&dlsi=592d827b4a754e90&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=1588e019b9e8a128bd2590a5ec19b4be9f93faf0)🌳 [Komorebi](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/0L8dak6CDIECehyShf2Vhb?si=ea2801aeecab4d6a&nd=1&dlsi=592d827b4a754e90&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=277fb11f481e67262705693e35c53d9cbd4b8bd4), 🕺 [I Choose Me](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/5iGe0KI1Nbk8Yd9cF5Yf99?si=b616567786004f5f&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=2c252f5744541f35c1cd9ceef14309c0544d8b6f), not only could I remember how I was feeling when I listened to those songs but also where I was in life, and most importantly how much I’ve grown since.

And it’s because of this growth that eventually led me to make the decision to close this chapter of my life.

This month’s theme is 🫶 [**Ending the Newsletter in the Most Beautiful Way I Know How**](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/7IqhwRWcoJSE1SLODoS0vX?si=249cdd57c55447dc&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=6d13ea133337808a08ba6c148392d7ed08e1e18c)**.**

If you’ve been subscribed for a while, you’ll know that I have always ended the year in a similar fashion (for more see [Part 1](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4VcLYn7O0CxMG5cAxuoMe0?si=4fa3bc390e6e4389&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=9d1b6e161ddacf7b0e6d7f86395264441cb86a3f), [Part 2](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/79TFc0aobJuj2yhvHljBzv?si=05e969c2e3314b4a&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=4786bef4b649640f495ace0dc8bbde84948df686), [Part 3](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/6pm2bEGu4RS38gwzPspu9w?si=9cd64a42599a47c6&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=f3338ecd9ec41ea0be7c51ccf7e51c57b187f1eb), [Part 4](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/2Xl8L2jAvkajgvli7QHDkK?si=50d4cb4789f44cea&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=bacfea9489acf8071cf852b26886f00695af4982), or [Part 5](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/1nq2G5VWPXVfz8PfEQD7UC?si=08a319672ca14455&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-final-issue&_bhlid=b65f62a3b98acef8e38a90107deec5f060ce3d6a)).

The simple truth is that this is the kind of music that I used to always come home to. It’s where my lonely, lost, and sad self found a warm blanket in.

It’s also the soundtrack to my safe wandering that eventually allowed me to find a little light in the darkness.

And it’s that little light, and trusting and surrendering to where it was guiding me, that allowed me to find more love, joy, and peace in my life.

My only hope in this life is to bring more of that to myself, to you, and to others.

I hope you enjoy it.
