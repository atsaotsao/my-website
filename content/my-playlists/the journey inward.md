Sometimes I like to start my curation process with one song.

I like to pick a destination and see what songs I can pick along the way to get me there.

When I heard Jamie xx’s [new track](https://open.spotify.com/track/1Cc00pWEDEDiA4eEv3npjD?si=b728b600d03a45d0&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-journey-inward), I immediately thought to myself: “This is where I want to travel to. It’s just so good.”

I start the playlist off with lighter and brighter tones as well as a slower tempo, with the likes of artists like [ford.](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7ItbAZITSFxSy5LJChXe18?si=kMYJC72BRFGAtsQtv2pO6Q&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-journey-inward), [ODESZA](https://open.spotify.com/artist/21mKp7DqtSNHhCAU2ugvUw?si=9_8WwufTTZu5eiKHEy8glg&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-journey-inward), and [Alex Lustig](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5oLxJrktO7kOEJANS6nkZB?si=3mNLjN_8RJ-oh3rGgz-b1Q&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-journey-inward).

As we travel farther and deeper, electronic music styles, tempos, and even tones will also start to shift and evolve.

Things may get a little heavier and faster, and you might feel like you’re flying through an asteroid field.

Right before we get to our destination, artists [Juelz](https://open.spotify.com/artist/04p1jV00pBnrmh2rKl0jnT?si=iACO8O5hTFGu42B7aPzKHw&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-journey-inward) and [RL Grime](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5eIbEEQnDM8yuDVB0bimSP?si=V4izi85uQq6olYRt989rTA&utm_source=25on25.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=the-journey-inward) will try to knock your engines out with their fierce trap creations.

Feel free to hop off at any point of this journey.

But if you have the courage and resilience to persist, I can promise you that I will bring you back to a place of safety and peace.

Regardless of where you are, I will bring you home.
