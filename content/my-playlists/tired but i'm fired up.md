I’ve been reading Rick Rubin’s [_The Creative Act: A Way of Being_](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/60965426) and it made me think about how I’ve approached the creative act of curating these playlists. It is always an incredibly personal process for me. When I’m selecting the songs to include, then playing around with the order in which I want to showcase them, I’m often thinking about not just the vibe I want to convey, but also the emotions, words, energy, and love that I want to share. I think about friends and loved ones in my life hoping that the intentions will land and that they will benefit from the messages conveyed through the curation of music. I think about the listeners I’ve never met IRL and the gratitude and joy I have for them for simply tuning in and connecting on this wavelength. I even think about my younger self, and try to pass on the messages to assure him that he will be okay. I also use it to convey to my partner the things that words sometimes just can’t do justice. My playlists are a part of me, so thank you again for being here. 

This month’s playlist includes a lot of tracks that have inspired me recently—they remind me to keep going even when there are always going to be hard days. They remind me to bring light to where there’s darkness and to always fight for the things that truly matter.
