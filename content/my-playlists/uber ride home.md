On the Uber ride home, I was an absolute mess. Still overwhelmed with emotion, the thought of not being home and not traveling with my girlfriend after so much anticipation was enough to make me tear up in the backseat. I was grateful for the darkness at 1am that enshrouded me and provided just the slightest of privacy from the driver at that moment. I was even more grateful that the driver was silent the entire drive. Instead, there was just music. It took me a second before realizing that he was not playing Top 40s or rap or any of the music fare you’d hear on your average Uber ride. They were songs I had never heard in my life and would never expect to see on any of Spotify’s playlists nor be algorithmically recommended to me; they had me completely hypnotized. Two songs in particular had imbued the atmosphere around me with such a sense of calm that they immediately transported me away from the low place I had sunk to and made me feel better. When I finally arrived back at my apartment, I politely asked him for the song names and made sure I tipped him directly on the app as he drove away.

For this month’s playlist, I took two songs in particular I’d heard on the Uber ride home that evoke the varied layers of emotions I was feeling that night and created an entire playlist around them. Bookended by my recent revelations, I started the playlist with the first song I heard called _“Shh” by Blank & Jones_ and ended it with the second, _“Malaguena Fantasia” by Stevan Pasero & Richard Patterson_.

My ultimate hope is that by allowing yourself to listen to the 25 songs curated to be listened to in order, you may also find yourself transported to a better and happier place during this wonderful albeit hectic holiday season which, as we know, is anything but conventional this year.

Happy Thanksgiving!
