I still don't always believe I deserve this love.
The love that makes my toes tingle,
my chest expand like an inflated futon,
my back shudder with a warm chill that flows, 
up and down, up and down.

How can I be so lucky,
and why don't I no longer need to do, perform, work to earn this?
What mind games are you playing with me?

I still don't always believe I deserve this peace.
The peace where my heart doesn't need to beat fast,
my shoulder can relax like the drooping leaves of the willow tree.
The knots from me & my ancestors can finally loosen,
here & there, here & there.

Why am I the one that gets to experience this?
and when are you taking this away from me?
What disaster is looming in the corner?

I still don't always believe I deserve this life.
The life where I get to smile every day,
my body can safely melt like *Aiyu* *jelly* in cold water,
my home messy with traces of 
people I love, people who love me.

I don't want to doubt this anymore,
because I'm tired of running,
so I let myself stay — just a little longer.
