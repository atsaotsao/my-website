I remember now as a baby
loud, unpredictable sounds rung in my ear from every direction 
I can hear every ding, every bang 
but I was helpless to cover them. 
I didn’t even have the warm bosoms of my mom to rest on. 
I feel it like the 3D projections of my young infantile self 
parallaxes against the ones who came before me.
the ones that still reside in my body and my spirit.

The ones that didn’t also have a voice to call out 
so they fought, they raged 
mostly at themselves because that was easier 
easier than feeling the full weight of the whole world.
of the wars. of the crackle of the fire as books, elders were burned.
of the rustling of the ships navigating the treacherous water toward Formosa.
of the sacrifices, the blood sweat and tears it took 
to even get the first generation of kids to the West. 
not knowing that all that work left us, our lineage, feeling more disconnected from the Mother.

After decades
We still couldn’t run away from the nightmares that haunt our souls.

I’m still finding my way back. 
For all of us 
to the place where we can finally rest,
lean on that steady trunk
nest our heads on the warmth of Her
look out to the serene waters where fireflies flew and beavers built,
and remember what it feels like to be completely free.
