There are days when hearing your voice
is the best thing that’s happened to me.

I thank the gods for making my ears sensitive
the engineers who built this thing in my hand
the cell tower that sends packets of ones and zeroes
across the ocean floor through threads of light

Just to hear your first “hi”
drip through like lemon colored custard
flowing out of steamy *liu sa bao*
filing my body with sweet, yolky warmth of you.

It melts away the hardest of aches.

I may not be the type of person thats motivated by food
but in those moments, I understood how my ancestors survived
because sometimes just a bite, just a “hi”
is enough for us to know

It’s all going to be okay.
