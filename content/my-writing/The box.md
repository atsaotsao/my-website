Imagine if there’s a box,
In this box is the reason why,
Things couldn’t work with,
One of most important loves of your life,
And yet this is a box that no one dares to look at except for you,
So it’s just you and the secrets of the box,
What do you do?

What do you do with the loneliness of knowing what could have been? 
What do you do with seeing the snake underneath the patterns of he says she says?
The pointing fingers the blame,
That all along it was beyond just,
Attachment patterns, communication issues
Beyond the hundreds of therapy hours me her we amassed 
That there is and was always something we both missed?

I can’t tell you whats in the box 
I cant tell you whats in the box without hearing her telling me I have not taken accountability. That I’m still focusing on her.
I can’t tell you whats in the box without 
hearing parts of me telling me to just move on, theres no point anymore. It’s not yours to care or carry.
I can’t tell you whats in the box without feeling the deep ocean of sadness because just touching the box makes my heart ache. 

I don’t know how to tell you whats in the box without using language that was not mine to begin with.
Thar there are these letters that don’t mean anything but it’s the closest I can get 
Letters  like
ADHD 
OCD 
OCPD
CPTSD
I C U
U C
ME?

Unlike pandora, I don’t need to open the box 
Not all boxes are meant to be opened and I get it now 
And I guess before I set the box down I just needed you to know
That I’m sorry 
And I’m ready to go now.
