You just got here,
why are you leaving already?

Stay and play a little longer,
I'll share all my treats & secrets with you.

We'll hopscotch through the moss beds,
dance under the moonlight
play tag among the redwood trees.
You can hide and I'll seek.

We'll sing along with the birds,
swim with the tadpoles,
pretend we're eagles together.
I can lead and you can follow.

I know they're coming to pick you up soon,
and I also have to go in just a second.

but just one more game, I promise.
We'll just have to make it count.
