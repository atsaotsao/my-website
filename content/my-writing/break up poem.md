I sat as my cat purred
sat as the sun peeked
through the leaves
drip by
drip.

I cracked opened the doors to the balcony
cracked my aching shoulder blades 
felt the loosening of the spine
little by
little.

I closed my eyes as the tremors of last night continued
closed the box that once held a ring
and I cried
bit by
bit.

I remembered the morning dew when I was on my knees
remembered the drunken kiss on 9th street
I felt a bittersweet smile rise
curve by 
curve.

I walked out of the door with my feet light
walked out of a castle I once felt safe in
out into the lush forest
step by
step.
