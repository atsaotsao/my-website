 A reminder to myself to breathe. This is the first time in a while where unexpectedly, even after a powerful start of morning routine that encouraged stillness on a beautiful Saturday morning, external factors caused the energy levels in the house to steer toward negative and no matter what things were being said, clarified, or repeated, it feels like being caught in a negative spiral where the parties involved all feel frustrated at the end.

It’s days like this that I know that there will always be bad days. It’s unavoidable. And bad days make it feel like progress isn’t being made, and that that this is always how things are going to be. It’s so easy to let these moments get to you, and I can still feel some of the fumes coming out of nostrils as I’m typing this, but it’s comforting to know I have a simple act of writing to get close resetting, to letting go, and move on to allow my day pick back up again.

It’s 9/11 after all, a day that reminds me not of the terror that took place, but the resilience of people especially after moments that challenge us.
