Over these past months I've been finding all these pianos to play on. 

Whether its this one just out and about in Nevada City with the summer sun blasting on it, with few keys that wouldn't work. Or a completely polished one in my loved one's family's study; neglected but surprisingly still in tune and loved how it echoed through the tall ceilings. Or a hot red one in a hotel lobby under the foot of Huashan Mountain in China, where its roped off and completely off-limits, apparently just for show (until I showed up). Or on an off-tune one in a community home in the midst of the celebration for mugwort & the Dragon Boat Festival, while people ate breakfast and took mugwort foot baths.

I find playing on them so comforting because maybe for the first time in a long time I find myself in the season traveling and transitioning between cities, neighborhoods, rooms, and beds. And the piano feels like a waypoint, a time-travel portal, that allows me to find one constant through the tectonic shifts of change. 

And at any point in time, I can travel to and from any one of them, like how dead wizards can in their own paintings in Harry Potter. I can even play a note and find myself back to the warm wooden childhood piano where my mom taught her students. The piano that I realized started it all.  "No one knows me like the piano, in my mothers home", Sampha sings.

And I never knew it would take me here of all places.
Here drenching in sweat from the heatwave in a subleased apartment for the month, and excited to share more of my colors, notes, and playing. The way my ancestors dreamed of. 

And here, getting ready for my upcoming art show at [T.O.L.K](https://www.instagram.com/tolk.nyc/?hl=en) starting in the second week of July.
It's my first time ever showing all my works in person, so strap-in, stay-tuned. I will be sharing more very soon.

As always, thank you for being here ❤️
