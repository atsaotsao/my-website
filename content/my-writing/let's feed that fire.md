Do you feel your creative ember but struggle to feed it?
Keep reading.

Ever since I've left my tech job in 2020, I've been slowly learning that creativity isn't just about making things. 

It's not just about the painting, the pottery, the improv, or the next class we sign up for (although those can be tremendously beautiful doorways)

The real *work* is deeper. 
And much, much more rewarding, nourishing, and soul-filling.

The systems we are operating in has taught us that creativity is a commodity. 
That it's about the product and´ outcome. Rather than the journey and process.

And even if you embraced the joys of expression, that it's designated to be a hobby.

So many of us circle our creativity without ever letting ourselves drop into it. 
We stay near the heat instead of stepping into the fire.

There are also many of us who are disconnected from it altogether.
And the thing about fire is that when you don't feed it. It slowly fades and fades after the years of neglect.

But I also have good news for you. 

Wherever you are on your creativity journey, the fire will never go away.
It's always there deep within you, an ember.

Perhaps waiting for the right time to finally ask you to listen. But when that times comes, will you listen?

I know for many of us, we are starting to feel the tectonic plates shifting underneath our feet. 

Whether its rise in fascism, climate change, AI, witnessing genocides, or experiencing the humaness of job loss, of heartbreak, of accompanying death of our loved ones

In these moments of upheaval is often when the ember and the fire wants to burn the brightest.

It's when your inner creative force and spirit, wants to remind you that your deepest desires and dreams matter. And that you are supported by the land you're on, the ancestors and the ones who came before you, and most importantly, by all parts and multitudes of you. 

And that it's possible to build new realities with a whole web & ecosystem of folks who also believe in dancing in the warmth of that collective creative field.

If you have been looking for a sign or permission slip, and have struggled to give that to yourself, let this be it. 

Let this be a collective invitation for us to feed that fire.
