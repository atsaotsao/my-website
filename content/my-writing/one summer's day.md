Sometimes my unconscious speaks to me in a song or melody.

While I was training in Taichung and my first week being stationed at the fire department, the opening track to the movie Spirited Away “One Summer’s Day”, keeps playing in my head. It always made me curious what is trying to speak to me about what I’m moving through in my life. 

I think about how potent the scene is of Chihuro crossing into and through a tunnel, a threshold and eventually into a new world where her parents, her name taken away from her ( renamed her “Sen”) and forced to exist and work in a new environment that ran on greed and contracts. 

I feel the weight, difficulties, but even the gift of this season’s liminal space and continuing feeling and accepting that there’s no walking back through the way I came. It’s hard right now that I’m in an environment where I don’t have the a lot of support for my rich complex emotions, dont have intimacy needs met easily, and probably most difficult is being confined in space 24 hours 5 days a week where dont have usual access to my tools of moving through these things or something as simple as taking a walk to touch a tree. But I also know the way is through. 

I am also feeling deeper in love. I continue feel the presence of guidance of spirit and my ancestors. The cafe I’m writing this literally played the ending track Itsumo Nandi Demo as I started writing this. I want to write more long form. I also started practicing DJing among all various forms of play, but more on that later. 

I hope you are well.
