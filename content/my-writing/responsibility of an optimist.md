 Before I truly understood what my optimism meant, where it came from, and how to harness it in the things I do, I always remember feeling very misunderstood. In fact, I always remembered that growing up it was easier to be a pessimist than an optimist. Not only was it more "edgy" and "cool", there always seemed to be more evidence that supported it: images of crime, grief, and tragedies perpetuated by media and popular culture. In addition, there always seemed to be someone around the corner ready to shut down any optimistic comment I had. Before I found the language to talk about the complexity of it, it was easy for my actions, words, and thoughts to be labeled as "corny", "cheesy" or even "romantic" and "unrealistic."

Fast-forward today, I still feel like there are more reasons ever to be a pessimist than an optimist. It's easy to point at the large macro problems that are dominating our headlines and psyches from climate change to war in Ukraine, or from the pandemic to rise in misinformation/increase in political polarities. If you were at a party and you said something like "2020 sucked" or some nihilistic comment like "the world is going to shit," many people will nod and agree with you. But no matter how I try, I will never be that guy. 

I realize that my optimism comes from a place of privilege but most importantly, is growing up in an environment where I truly felt just a lot of love. It's this love that propelled me to believe that anything is possible, and it the same love that allowed me to see how my optimism can help contribute to the healing of individuals who are not only struggling but also holding to pessimistic world views that make their journey feel even more lonely and arduous. 

As an optimist, I now often feel like I have a responsibility to bring others to this side. It's not because I want others to accept this worldview, but I genuinely believe that most can benefit with at least healthy dose of positivity and optimism in their lives, and I am starting by injecting as much of it in my daily coaching.
