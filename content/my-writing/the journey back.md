The haziness of powerlessness is a potent elixir.
Drink it, and ironically, I feel immortal.

I feel the void flowing into and through the body,
like the polar opposite of what spinach probably does for Popeye,
and feel my limbs adrift into familiar lands. 

Like a raft bringing me closer to the islands,
of aloneness of withdrawal and of depression.

I see the ancient ruins of the times I resided there,
finding sanctuary beneath the bricks and stones once again.
And I see the allure of starting a fire and staying for a while. 
Whats the harm?

Time, feelings, responsibilities, and all dimensions that bring me overwhelm all stop.
There may not be peace,
but there is a twisted shape of choice 
And somehow a smidge of power in choosing it.

But when the shades are closed, the anchors dropped.
It brings me to a whirlpool of infinity. 
And it’s here that I learned that one can always go deeper.
To fall deeper into the abyss,
deeper into the adapted comforts of known frigid waters. 

Between known chills and unknown warmth, 
somehow my brain and body tricks me into accepting the former. 
The world taught me to pick that road. 
The road less traveled, a Frosty choice they say.
The steps to death seem less scary by each meter.
At least I can still turn around and see my footprints.

It’s in the negative infinity that I started to wonder,
like a ray of sunshine piercing into a pitch black well,
If this darkness can be this deep and infinite, maybe it’s true the other way around too.
What if we went that way?

So I gathered what remained of the battered pieces of the raft, 
Strap them together with the remaining drips of strength left in my arms
and started rowing. 

Maybe this time around,
I’ll survive the journey.
