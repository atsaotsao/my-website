I'm scared of not knowing.
I'm scared of regrets, that little voice that likes to tease me with the "what ifs?"
I'm scared of not being happy.
I'm scared of being back there in the well of shame, victimhood, and anguish.
I'm scared of arriving at the edge of things again, and looking down to see darkness that slowly starts to look inviting.
I'm scared of being back to the place where I didn't yet learn how to turn on my flashlight so I can see a way out.
I'm scared of looking around and thinking that no one was around to catch me if I trip, stumble, and fall.
I'm scared of the feeling of coming back to my warm bed at the end of a long day, have closed my eyes, yet still don't feel like I'm at home.
I'm scared of the cries of my body that never got to step foot in the lands where my ancestors were from.
I'm scared of anger and grief I still carry that were the result having our family tree unrooted violently, and tossed to a new soil while expecting to grow quickly.
I'm scared of the deep innate fear of not feeling safe right as I exited the womb.
I'm scared of being angry at the caretakers of the world neglecting their duties to make this little boy feel safe.
I'm scared that the world loved me more if I stay scared.
I'm scared of admitting how much I don't want to be scared again.
I'm scared of worrying about being around people who also feels scared.
I'm scared to admit how much I take on someone else's emotions.
I'm scared to be with the fact that my body is still scared and that's just where I'm at.
I'm scared to be slow in the race toward fearlessness.
I'm scared to be left behind.
I'm scared of losing.
I'm scared of dying.
I'm scared of living.

But I don't want to be scared anymore.
