Hand me the rope
and let me hang.
hang free, hands free,
At least I get to choose.

Go ahead and tighten the noose,
I'm not scared.
I've been here before,
he & I are friends, you see.

Loosen the rope,
and we can jump with it.
Like we did as kids,
on the playground one two three.

Tie me up,
but don't tie me down.
I need to know I’m safe,
even when I'm all wrapped up.

You can even tie my hands behind me,
and do what you want with me, I won’t put up a fight.
As long as when it ends,
you cuddle me and tell me "it's all going to be alright."
