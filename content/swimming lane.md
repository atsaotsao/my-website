Right and left arm, I head down the lane
Every three, I take a breath to keep myself going
With my hurt back, this activity keeps me sane
It's such a joy, to see my lung capacity growing
But at the end of the day, I'm still frustrated with the pain.

The pain in my upper back robbed me of the joy of a climb
The hours I would spend progressing these physical obstacles
I miss it, but I know that there's always a moment, a time
Where we learn our biggest lessons about our bodies, the fragility of our particles

It's a humbling lesson, one that comes with age
I don't need to feel older, but revel in the wisdom
Of breaking free from the past; all the trauma, the struggle, and the rage
And understanding that there are many wrongs with the system
And that I am not powerless. And I can help more than just myself break out of the cage.
