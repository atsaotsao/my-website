There is always so many things I want to do that I don’t have time for.  
Time always seems like such a finite resource that I’m forever running out.

I used have these recurring dreams that I have the super-power to stop time.  
I remember the sad reality was that I was only using this superpower simply to get some more sleep, and to catchup on my homework.

But it doesn’t have to be like that and I’m learning.  
Time slipping away isn’t a tragedy, its just a nature course of life.  
And I’m learning to accept it.
